Interactive Odoo POS Prototype

Experience Odoo's high-performance enterprise hospitality UI. These screens use unified styles, responsive structures, and have been linked together to demonstrate real front-of-house & back-of-house interactions.

Front of House 2560 x 2048 (Desktop)

1. POS Order View - Terminal

Front-of-house cashier terminal where orders are placed. Features categories, grid layout of products, current guest's cart, totals summary, and actions to checkout or send order directly to the kitchen KDS.

Front of House 2560 x 2048 (Desktop)

2. Floor Plan & Table Selection

Table layout selector for hosts and cashiers. Displays Ground Floor, Outdoor, and Rooftop seating zones. Shows table numbers, capacity, available status, and occupied bills. Click on Table 5 to jump straight into its POS order.

Back of House 2560 x 2048 (Desktop)

3. Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Back-of-house kitchen station monitor. Renders incoming orders as culinary tickets, indicating elapsed wait times, dine-in vs. takeout status, and dish customisations. Interactive click-to-complete ticks keep chefs in sync.

Design System Specifications

The Odoo Inspired Design System is engineered to balance high legibility of dense back-office data grids with fast touch targets for front-of-house operations.

Theme: Light Radius: 4px–5px Grid: 12-Column Desktop Base Unit: 4px

Core Colors

  • Purple Heritage (#714B67)
  • Teal Primary (#017E84)
  • Deep Charcoal (#111827)
  • Canvas Surface (#F8F9FF)

Typography Hierarchy

  • Primary Face Inter (Systematic & high legibility)
  • Cursive Accents Caveat (Used on welcome titles & logo)
  • Line Height / Scaling Generous 1.5x ratio, scales perfectly

Depth & Touch Targets

  • Raised Level (L1) 0px 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.1) for cards
  • Touch Target Padding Minimum 44px–48px hit areas
  • Elevation Shift Subtle hover lifts to L2 shadow depth
